Understanding Sadaqah

"The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: 'The believer's shade on the Day of Resurrection will be his charity.'" - Tirmidhi

What is Sadaqah?

Sadaqah is voluntary charity given out of compassion, love, friendship, religious duty, or generosity. Unlike Zakat, which is obligatory, Sadaqah is given freely and can be given to anyone in need.

Benefits of Sadaqah

  • Purifies wealth and soul
  • Increases blessings in life
  • Protects from calamities
  • Earns rewards in the Hereafter
  • Strengthens community bonds

Types of Sadaqah

Financial Sadaqah

Giving money or material possessions to those in need

Sadaqah Jariyah

Continuous charity that benefits others even after your death, such as:

  • Building a mosque
  • Digging a well
  • Planting trees
  • Providing education

Non-Material Sadaqah

Acts of kindness that don't involve money:

  • Helping others
  • Sharing knowledge
  • Good words and advice
  • Smiling at others
