Understanding Zakat

"The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: 'Whoever pays the zakat on his wealth will have its evil removed from him.'" - Ibn Khuzaimah

How to Calculate Your Zakat

  1. Determine your zakatable assets (cash, gold, silver, investments, business assets)
  2. Subtract your short-term debts
  3. Check if your net wealth exceeds the nisab threshold
  4. Calculate 2.5% of your net zakatable wealth

Nisab Threshold

The nisab is the minimum amount of wealth one must have before being liable to pay zakat. It is equivalent to:

  • 87.48 grams of gold, or
  • 612.36 grams of silver
